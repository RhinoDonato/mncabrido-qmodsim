PROPOSED REPAIR WORKFLOW

Repair Workflow

The complete flow of a repair job, from device intake to release.

  1. Intake

    Register customer and device.

  2. Diagnosis

    Check the device and record findings.

  3. Quotation

    Prepare the repair cost and wait for approval.

  4. Repair

    Perform the repair and update status.

  5. Payment

    Record payments and remaining balance.

  6. Release

    Mark the device ready and release to customer.

Workflow details

Customer approval is recorded before repair proceeds. If parts are needed, staff can note requirements and sourcing progress per repair job. The technician tests the device before it is marked ready for release.
