About MNCabrido

MNCabrido is a local repair business in Barangay West Rembo, Taguig City. It provides repair and troubleshooting services for laptops, desktops, and printers, with the owner overseeing daily operations and repair records.

Why RepairOps?

It brings repair records and progress into one system, making shop operations more organized, easier to track, and more reliable than using paper forms and Excel.

Repair Workflow

The complete flow of a repair job, from device intake to release.

  1. Intake

    Register customer and device.

  2. →
  3. Diagnosis

    Check the device and record findings.

  4. →
  5. Quotation

    Prepare cost and wait for approval.

  6. →
  7. Repair

    Repair, test, and update status.

    Parts & testing

    Source parts when needed, record them per job, and test the device before release.

  8. →
  9. Payment

    Record payments and balance.

  10. →
  11. Release

    Mark ready and release to customer.

    Customer approval

    Quotation approval is recorded before repair proceeds. Ready-for-release updates can be sent by email, with SMS optional.

System Users and Roles

The system is used only by the shop’s staff for internal management.

Owner

Manages overall operations, users, records, payments, expenses, and reports.

Technician

Handles diagnosis, repair updates, parts required, and repair status.

Customer

Does not log in or book repairs. Receives ready-for-release email updates, with optional SMS.

Key Notes

Only staff access

The internal system is used only by the owner and technicians.

Ready-for-release updates

Customers receive an email when their device is ready. SMS is optional.

Parts per repair job

Parts are tracked per job, not as a full inventory system.
