MNCabrido iFix
RepairOps

Web-Based Repair Service Management and Monitoring System. A proposed system shaped around the shop’s current workflow and records.

The business

About MNCabrido

MNCabrido is a local repair business in Barangay West Rembo, Taguig City. It provides repair and troubleshooting services for laptops, desktops, and printers.

The owner oversees most operations, including the repair process and the records needed to manage the business.

LocationBarangay West Rembo, Taguig City
DevicesLaptops · Desktops · Printers
Current recordsJob order forms · Paper · Excel
Why RepairOps is proposed

Bring repair records and progress into one view.

Customer, device, repair, and transaction details currently span paper records and a self-developed Excel dashboard. The proposed web system aims to make those records easier to organize and repair progress easier to monitor.

01 · Records

Centralized job history

Keep customer, device, job order, diagnosis, repair notes, and status history connected in one record.

02 · Workflow

Clear repair progress

Track intake, diagnosis, quotation, approval, repair, and release—including jobs that need longer troubleshooting.

03 · Shop operations

Parts and payments

Record parts needed for each job, supplier and order status, plus charges, downpayments, balances, and payment status.

04 · Monitoring

Dashboards and updates

Give the owner repair and financial summaries, with planned customer status notifications when a device is ready for release.

Scope note. Parts are recorded per repair job; the proposal does not describe a general inventory system. Email is the initial notification channel, with SMS dependent on an available service.

Proposed repair workflow

From device intake to release.

  1. 01Intake & job order
  2. 02Diagnosis
  3. 03Quotation & approval
  4. 04Repair & parts
  5. 05Payment
  6. 06Release & update
Who the proposed system serves

Shop staff manage the records; customers receive updates.

Owner / Administrator

Oversees the business

Manages customers, job orders, staff assignments, payments, expenses, reports, and other shop records.

Technician

Records repair work

Views assigned jobs and records diagnosis, repair notes, parts needed, and repair status.

Customer

Receives important updates

No account or online booking is required. Staff record customer details at the shop; notifications can share key repair updates.
